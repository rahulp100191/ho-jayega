A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Clarity is part
of the service.

Is Ho Jayega only for NRIs?+

No. We support local families, professionals, business owners, relocating households and NRIs. You can choose one service or combine several.

How is my consultation confirmed?+

Choose your services, share your details and preferred time, then send the prepared request on WhatsApp. Our team confirms availability and shares the call details. Your requested time is not reserved until we confirm it.

How do fees and spending approvals work?+

We propose fees after understanding your requirements. Scope, third-party charges, purchase approvals and reporting expectations are agreed before work begins.

What does parent care include?+

Non-medical support, errands, check-ins and coordination with healthcare providers. Medical advice, nursing and emergency treatment must be provided by qualified professionals. For an immediate emergency, contact local emergency services.
