Security, privacy & compliance.

We will not invent certifications or make bank-grade claims before we earn them. Our launch standard is simple: collect less, share less, document access and never ask for credentials we should not have.

No OTPs, PINs or passwords

Ho Jayega will never ask you to share banking passwords, card PINs, UPI PINs or one-time passwords. Sensitive transactions remain under your control.

Consent-first document handling

Identity or property documents are requested only when a specific approved task requires them, and only the minimum information necessary should be shared.

Task-scoped access

Vendors or ground personnel should receive only the information needed for their active assignment, not your entire family file.

Expense transparency

Third-party spending is documented with invoices, receipts or payment evidence wherever available. Material expenses require approval before commitment where practical.

Professional boundaries

Doctors, nurses, lawyers, accountants and other regulated professionals remain responsible for their professional advice. Ho Jayega coordinates logistics and execution.

Privacy by design

We aim to retain only information needed to operate the service and will move sensitive family records into a controlled client portal as the platform matures.

Common questions.

Clear boundaries matter when the service concerns parents, health and property.

Is this a medical service?

No. We coordinate appointments, transport, hospital accompaniment and follow-ups. Diagnosis and treatment remain with licensed professionals.

Can my parents contact you directly?

Yes, depending on the plan and agreed scope, parents can use the same local WhatsApp/phone contact.

Can you manage a vacant property?

Yes. Inspections, photos, maintenance coordination and practical local follow-up can be included.

What about emergencies?

Members can use the emergency coordination line for ambulance, hospital and family escalation. Physical attendance depends on location, availability and plan coverage. We are not a replacement for ambulance, police or emergency medical services.

What if my parents are uncomfortable with the assigned person?

Tell us immediately. We will review the issue and change the relationship manager or operating arrangement where reasonably possible.

Can I cancel?

Monthly plans are intended to remain flexible. Any notice period, prepaid discount or task already in progress will be explained clearly before you subscribe.
