Common questions.

Clear boundaries matter when the service concerns parents, health and property.

Is this a medical service?

No. We coordinate appointments, transport, hospital accompaniment and follow-ups. Diagnosis and treatment remain with licensed professionals.

Can my parents contact you directly?

Yes, depending on the plan and agreed scope, parents can use the same local WhatsApp/phone contact.

Can you manage a vacant property?

Yes. Inspections, photos, maintenance coordination and practical local follow-up can be included.

What about emergencies?

We provide priority coordination and can arrange local transport or hospital logistics. We are not a replacement for emergency medical or police services.
