BEFORE WE BEGIN

A few details.
Clearly answered.

Is Ho Jayega right for me?+

We support CXOs, business owners, professionals, working couples, families, NRIs, older adults, homeowners and relocating households. If you need practical personal support, tell us what you’d like to delegate. We confirm suitability and scope together.

What does my dedicated manager do?+

Your manager is your primary point of contact. They learn your preferences, research options, coordinate providers, seek approvals, track completion, reconcile agreed expenses and keep the household record current. Specific responsibilities are agreed in your engagement.

How do pricing and approvals work?+

After a consultation, we propose the scope and service fees. Third-party costs and spending approvals are agreed separately. We can discuss a specific request or an ongoing arrangement.

How is my consultation confirmed?+

Select your services and preferred time, review your details and send the prepared request through WhatsApp. Our team confirms availability and shares the call details. Your time is reserved only after confirmation.

What are the limits of parent care?+

Parent care covers non-medical support and coordination. Medical advice, nursing and emergency treatment are provided by qualified professionals. For an immediate emergency, contact local emergency services.
