Yes. We can coordinate everyday local needs for parents and family members in Guwahati, subject to the task being safe, legal and within our service scope.

The founding pilot is focused on Guwahati. Availability can depend on the specific task, locality and service provider.

You do not have to search, compare and follow up with multiple providers yourself. You message one point of contact and we coordinate the task for you.
